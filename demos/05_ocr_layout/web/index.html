Drop a scanned invoice, a lab report or a bank statement in.

The left pane shows what the detector found. This pane shows three
readings of the same boxes:

  Layout preserved   boxes quantised onto a character grid derived
                     from the page's own median glyph advance, so
                     columns stay columns

  Flattened          the same boxes joined in reading order, which
                     is what a default OCR call gives you

  Tables             runs of lines whose cells land on the same
                     vertical anchors, pulled out as real grids
                     and exported to CSV or Excel

On a single-column page the first two agree. On anything with two
columns they do not, and the flattened one is quietly wrong.
Runs locally. PaddleOCR when installed, Tesseract otherwise. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
