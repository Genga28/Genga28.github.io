Drop a scanned invoice, a lab report or a bank statement in.

The left pane shows what the detector found. This pane shows two
reconstructions of the same boxes:

  Layout preserved   boxes quantised onto a character grid derived
                     from the page's own median glyph advance, so
                     columns stay columns and tables stay tables

  Flattened          the same boxes joined in reading order, which
                     is what you get from a default OCR call

On a single-column page they agree. On anything with two columns
they do not, and the flattened one is quietly wrong.
